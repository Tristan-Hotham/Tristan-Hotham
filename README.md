### Hi, I'm Dr Tristan Hotham

I'm a researcher on social media, campaigns and politics. I study how political parties, leaders and campaigns use Facebook and other platforms, and how audiences respond. My PhD (University of Bath, 2021) looked at how British political parties use Facebook in election campaigns.

**Website:** [tristanhotham.com](https://tristanhotham.com/) · **All papers:** [tristanhotham.com/research](https://tristanhotham.com/research/) · **ORCID:** [0000-0003-4931-9349](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-4931-9349) · **Google Scholar:** [profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RN6xlggAAAAJ) · **YouTube:** [@tristanhotham](https://www.youtube.com/@tristanhotham) · **Blog:** [blog.tristanhotham.com](https://blog.tristanhotham.com/)

#### What I work on

- Party and leader pages on Facebook, and who carries a party's online audience
- Satellite campaigns such as Momentum, and what I call Janus-faced campaigning
- Targeted political advertising and A/B testing
- Comment sections, incivility and toxicity
- Populism, the radical right and engagement across Europe
- Elections in Britain, Europe and the United States

I work with digital trace data (CrowdTangle, Netvizz, Facebook's Ad Library), hand coding and text-as-data methods. Where the terms of collection allow, I share aggregate data with each paper. Post-level data are not shared.
