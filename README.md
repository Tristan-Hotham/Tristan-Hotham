### Hi, I'm Dr Tristan Hotham

**Data-led researcher of politics, the past, platforms and places. Maker of maps, games and other things.**

I turn public data into interactive maps, open datasets and short papers: elections and MPs, the deep past, places, the sea and sky, and how parties campaign on Facebook.

#### Start here

- **[Who Won Here?](https://maps.tristanhotham.com/elections-since-1832/)** All 49 UK general elections from 1832 to 2024 on one map: 31,736 seats, the forgotten uncontested elections, the Liberal fall and the Red Wall.
- **[Where the Sun Sets into the Sea](https://maps.tristanhotham.com/sea-sunsets/)** 1,717 beaches in Britain and Ireland, day by day: 47% see a sunset into open sea on some day of the year, only 52 every day, and Arran hides 134 sunsets a year from Troon.
- **[Does a Local MP Get a Local Bonus?](https://maps.tristanhotham.com/local-mp-bonus/)** 31,737 MP returns since 1832: MPs born within 25 km of their seat win about 1.3 points more of the vote, but are defeated just as often.
- **[The Known World](https://maps.tristanhotham.com/known-world/)** 681 journeys from the Bronze Age to 1960 light up a dark world: Europe had recorded 20% of the land in 1492 and 67% by 1900, and much of the dark was known first to China, the Islamic world or Polynesian voyagers.
- **[The Wealth of Nations, Mapped](https://maps.tristanhotham.com/gdp-through-time/)** The world economy on one playable map from 10,000 BC to 2024: China and India made 45% of world output in 1820, 9% in 1950 and 30% today.
- **[Ptolemy's World, Measured](https://maps.tristanhotham.com/ptolemy/)** 2,358 of Ptolemy's places set against where they really are: shrink his longitudes by 0.72 and the map falls into place, but Scotland is turned on its side.

#### How to cite and reuse

Data and code are open. Each study repo has, or will soon have, a `CITATION.cff` (use "Cite this repository" on its GitHub page), and each study has a DOI on Zenodo. The licence is set per repo. Please cite the Zenodo DOI where you can.
